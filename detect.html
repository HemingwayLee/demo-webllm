How to fix it
  • Update your browser. WebGPU is on by default in Chrome/Edge 113+, Safari 26+, and Firefox 141+ (Windows) or 145+ (macOS on Apple Silicon).
  • Open this page over https:// or http://localhost. WebGPU is blocked on plain http:// pages and on file:// in some browsers.
  • Update your graphics drivers. Browsers turn WebGPU off for some older or blocklisted GPUs.
  • In Chrome, open chrome://gpu to see why WebGPU is unavailable.
